Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How quickly can you start my project?

We typically begin new projects within 1-2 weeks after the initial consultation and agreement. For urgent projects, we can often accommodate faster start times.

What is your development process?

We follow an agile methodology with four main phases: Discovery (understanding your needs), Strategy (planning the solution), Development (building with regular updates), and Launch & Support (deployment and ongoing maintenance).

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes! We offer 24/7 support packages and maintenance plans to ensure your application runs smoothly. We also provide training for your team and documentation for all our work.

Can you work with our existing team?

Absolutely! We're experienced in collaborating with in-house teams and can integrate seamlessly into your existing workflow. We can also provide staff augmentation services if needed.

What technologies do you specialize in?

We specialize in AI/ML, mobile development (iOS with Swift, Android with Kotlin, and Flutter for cross-platform), web development (React, Next.js, Vue.js), and UI/UX design. We stay current with the latest technologies to provide the best solutions.

How do you ensure project security and confidentiality?

We take security seriously. All projects are covered by NDAs, we use secure development practices, implement enterprise-grade security measures, and ensure compliance with industry standards like GDPR and HIPAA where applicable.
