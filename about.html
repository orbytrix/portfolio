About OrbyTrix

We're a team of passionate innovators dedicated to transforming businesses through AI-powered solutions and cutting-edge technology.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, OrbyTrix emerged from a simple yet powerful vision: to make advanced technology accessible to businesses of all sizes. What started as a small team of three developers has grown into a thriving company of 50+ talented professionals serving clients worldwide.

Our journey began when we noticed a critical gap in the market. Small and medium-sized businesses were struggling to compete in an increasingly digital world, lacking access to the sophisticated AI tools and custom software that larger corporations enjoyed. We set out to change that.

Today, we specialize in creating AI-powered assistants that work like a CEO for your business, mobile applications that delight users, and web platforms that drive growth. Our expertise spans iOS, Android, Flutter, and cutting-edge web technologies, always with a focus on beautiful UI/UX design.

We've delivered over 500 projects, maintained a 98% client satisfaction rate, and helped businesses save thousands of hours through intelligent automation. But what we're most proud of is the lasting relationships we've built with our clients, many of whom have grown alongside us.
